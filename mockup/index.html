THE 7 MONTHLY GOALS

    PLANT = HEALTH

    Every member and every group has a pixel plant. It grows from seed to full bloom as goals are completed, and droops when a member is falling behind, so you can see at a glance who needs encouragement.

    GROUP GOAL

    Baptisms are tracked as a shared group goal with water drops filling in as the group reaches it, plus encouragement messages with Bible verses to keep everyone pushing.
